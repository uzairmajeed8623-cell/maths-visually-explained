The question

A triangle has two sides of lengths \(7\,\text{cm}\) and \(9\,\text{cm}\). Their included angle is \(60^\circ\). Find the opposite side \(a\), to three significant figures.

Worked solution

1. Choose the cosine rule

We know two sides and the angle between them, so use:

\[a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A\]

2. Substitute the values

\[\begin{aligned} a^2 &= 7^2+9^2-2\times7\times9\cos60^\circ\\ &= 49+81-63\\ &= 67 \end{aligned}\]

3. Take the square root

\[a=\sqrt{67}\approx8.19\,\text{cm}\]

The final answer is \(8.19\,\text{cm}\), to three significant figures.

A common mistake

Do not use Pythagoras here: the given angle is \(60^\circ\), not a right angle.
