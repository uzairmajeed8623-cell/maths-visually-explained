Question 16

\(OBCD\) is a rectangle. \(DCE\) is a straight line.

\(B\) has coordinates \((2,-4)\).
\(E\) has coordinates \((12,-6.5)\).

Work out the coordinates of \(D\). You must show all your working.

Worked solution

1. Find the gradient of OB

\(O\) is the origin, so \(O=(0,0)\).

\[ m_{OB}=\frac{-4-0}{2-0}=-2 \]

2. Find the equation of DE

Opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel. Since \(D\), \(C\) and \(E\) lie on a straight line, \(DE\) is parallel to \(OB\), so its gradient is also \(-2\).

Substitute \(E=(12,-6.5)\) into \(y=-2x+c\):

\[ \begin{aligned} -6.5&=-2(12)+c\\ c&=17.5 \end{aligned} \]

Therefore, the equation of \(DE\) is

\[y=-2x+17.5.\]

3. Find the equation of OD

Adjacent sides of a rectangle are perpendicular. The gradient of \(OD\) is the negative reciprocal of \(-2\):

\[m_{OD}=-\frac{1}{-2}=\frac12.\]

Since \(OD\) passes through the origin, its equation is

\[y=\frac12x.\]

4. Solve the equations to find D

At \(D\), the two lines intersect. Equate their expressions for \(y\):

\[ \begin{aligned} -2x+17.5&=\frac12x\\ -4x+35&=x\\ 35&=5x\\ x&=7 \end{aligned} \]

Substitute \(x=7\) into \(y=\frac12x\):

\[y=\frac12\times7=3.5.\]

Therefore, the coordinates of \(D\) are

\[\boxed{D=(7,\,3.5)}\]
